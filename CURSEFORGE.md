# RXP ForeverTracker

**A scrollable, smarter quest tracker for WoW Forever, with your RestedXP guide built right in.**

New in **3.1**: a quest item bar, big progress messages, a world marker over your quest, and a Guides section. Also new since 2.0: a brand-new settings window, statistics with run analysis, and a first-time setup that walks you through every feature.

## Getting started

1. Install and log in.
2. A short **first-time setup** opens. Turn each feature on or off.
3. Type **/qt** any time to open the settings. **/qt setup** opens the setup again.

## Quest tracker

- **Mouse-wheel scrolling** for Blizzard's own tracker, with an optional scrollbar.
- **Zone sorting**: quests under zone headers, the closest first, with distance arrows.
- **Rewards at a glance**: experience and gold after each title, progress bars, a check on finished quests, and rich tooltips.
- **Icons** for elite, group, dungeon, raid, PvP and new-in-Forever quests.
- **3D model panel**: hover an objective that names an NPC or object to see its model.
- **Dungeon card**: in a dungeon or raid, its name, party, deaths, instances this hour and your lockout.
- **Rares near your quests** on the zone header, with distance and a waypoint.
- **Find a group**: Ctrl+click a quest to post LFM or list it in Looking For Group.
- **Wowhead link**: right-click a quest for its WoW Forever Wowhead URL.

## Helpers

- **Quest item bar** with a key binding (Key Bindings > AddOns > RXP ForeverTracker).
- **Big progress messages**: "Wolves slain: 3/8" in large letters, and "Ready to turn in".
- **World marker**: your tracked quest's name, next step, distance and a light beam over its spot. Can track the nearest quest by itself.
- **Quest items marked in your bags**, with a warning if you sell or delete one.
- **Best reward marker**: a gold coin on the reward that sells for the most.
- **Quest close by** (Questie): a card when a quest you can take is near. Breadcrumb quests you do not need are left out.
- **Party chat** (off by default): say when a quest is done, or each step.
- **Talking to NPCs** (off until you turn it on): accept quests, turn in finished ones, and skip a one-option chat. Hold Shift to pause it.
- **Breath warning** and **flight times**.

## Guides

- **Legacy Challenges**: every challenge, with what is still to do.
- **Library books**: done, in your bags or open. Click one for a waypoint. (Questie)
- **Cozy Sleeping Bag** chain from level 14. Click a step for a waypoint. (Questie)

## Statistics

- **Overview**: quests done, experience from quests, quest times, quests per hour, top zones.
- **Run analysis**: where your time went, and time per level.
- **Level splits**: time per level, compared with another character.
- **Copy for Discord** and a level-up line in chat.

## RestedXP guide (needs RestedXP Guides)

- Current and upcoming steps in the tracker, with click-to-use buttons, a waypoint arrow and back / restart / forward buttons.
- **Prerequisite Journal** (with Questie): what you are missing for quests ahead in your guide.
- **"Why did guide skip?"** (/qt why) and **orphaned quest cleanup**.

## Optional addons

- **RestedXP Guides**: guide steps in the tracker.
- **Questie**: quest locations, chains, party progress, nearby quests, library books and the sleeping bag chain.
- **DungeonJournal** and **NovaInstanceTracker**: extra lines on the dungeon card.

## Commands

- **/qt**: settings
- **/qt setup**: first-time setup
- **/qt stats**, **/qt run**: statistics
- **/qt help**: every command
